### 💡 Pro Tip: Find the RSS feed of ANY website
Most blogs, news sites, and platforms automatically have an RSS feed built-in. You can usually find it by adding one of these endings to the website's main link:
* `/feed` (e.g., `https://example.com/feed`)
* `/rss` (e.g., `https://example.com/rss`)
* `/feed.xml` or `/rss.xml`
